# **App Name**: Apex Vista

## Core Features:

- Property Listings: Display real estate property listings with detailed information and high-quality images.
- Solar Panel Visualizer: Allow users to upload images of their properties and visualize how solar panels would look on their roofs.
- Digital Marketing Packages: Showcase different digital marketing packages tailored for real estate and solar panel businesses.
- Lead Capture Form: Implement a form to capture leads interested in real estate, solar panels, or digital marketing services.
- AI-Powered Content Suggestions: Suggest personalized real-estate or digital marketing content (descriptions, ad copy, social media posts) based on property characteristics and marketing goals using an AI tool.
- Testimonials Section: Display customer testimonials to build trust and credibility.
- Contact Form: Users will be able to leave a message with the form and send them.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep sky blue (#3498db) for trustworthiness and modernity.
- Background color: Light gray (#f0f2f5) for a clean and professional look.
- Accent color: Orange (#e67e22) to highlight calls to action and important information.
- Headline font: 'Poppins', a geometric sans-serif.
- Body font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif
- Modern UI layout with clear sections for each service: real estate, solar panels, and digital marketing.
- Subtle motion graphics powered by Next.js motion to create an engaging user experience.
